# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free project space. Participants must be professional, constructive, and respectful of differing opinions and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and publication of private information without permission. Critique code, claims and evidence—not people.

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private contact/reporting mechanism after publication.

This is an initial project policy and should be reviewed before the public launch.
